My Money and (AI)

Your bank, card and UPI alerts, turned into a private spending dashboard — built on your phone, kept on your phone.

Reads your alerts

Turns transactional SMS from Indian banks, cards, UPI apps, EPFO and NPS into a clean list of what you spent and earned. OTPs, offers and personal messages are ignored.

Fills the gaps from Gmail

Optional: link Gmail with read-only access and the app adds bank alert emails whose SMS never arrived. If an SMS already has the transaction, the email is skipped.

Private by design

No My Money server, no account, no ads, no analytics. Everything is processed and stored on your device. Backups go only where you choose.

Budgets, bills & goals

Categories, budgets, upcoming bills, reminders, savings goals and an optional AI coach that sees only anonymised totals.

How we use Google data

When you link Gmail, the app requests the read-only gmail.readonly permission and searches only for bank and payment alert emails. They are downloaded to your phone and processed there — never sent to us, never used for ads, never read by a person. Google Drive backup uses only a hidden app folder in your own Drive. Our use of Google data follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Details: Privacy Policy, section 1.3.
